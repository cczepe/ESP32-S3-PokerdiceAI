# Changelog

Version numbers: a fix raises the last digit (1.0.1), a new feature the middle one (1.1.0).
The firmware has its own version number (`firmware/main/version.h`).

## 1.0.0

First public version.

- **Firmware 1.0.0** for ESP32-S3 camera boards: detection of poker dice with two int8 models (ESP-DL), poker hand,
  web page (play, history, monitoring, settings, snapshot), camera for the trainer (live view, single images,
  fixed or auto exposure), home Wi-Fi or own hotspot with captive portal, status LED.
- **Trainer 1.0.0**: step-by-step web program for your own dice - camera setup, die measurement and photos, die layout,
  tray, felt, surroundings, real test throws with labelling, optional AI helpers (Anthropic API).
- **Colab**: Blender rendering of synthetic throws, training of detector (ESPDet-Pico) and classifier, export;
  five notebooks generated by `trainer/colab/build_notebooks.py`.
- **Conversion** for the ESP32 with esp-ppq, evaluation on the real test images, `select_project.py` for the firmware.
- Example models in `firmware/models/`.
