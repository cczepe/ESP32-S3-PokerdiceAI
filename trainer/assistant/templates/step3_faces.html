What for? These 6 photos become the textures of the 3D die in Blender: each face exactly as it looks on your die. Here you also define the symbols (name, kind, colour). This gives the class list the model learns.

  1. Put the die on the green felt (or a dark, matt surface) - not on white paper, it can hardly be told apart from the die. Ruler optional.
  2. Phone straight above it, 30-40 cm, 2× zoom, tap on the die; no flash.
  3. One photo per face: turn the die so that the next face is on top. 6 photos in total.
  4. Check here: the trainer marks the die (yellow box). If the box is wrong, drag a new one: with the mouse from one corner of the die to the opposite one.
  5. Click “🤖 Suggest symbols”, check and correct the suggestions, then “Confirm symbols”.

Pay attention to:

  • Sharp and bright - the photos are the template for all training images.
  • No shadow of hand or phone on the die, no reflection.
  • Do not put the die at the image border, do not let the ruler touch it.
  • The rotation of the symbol on the photo does not matter - you set it in step 4 in the die net.

Typical mistakes: photographed at an angle (the die looks like a trapezoid), flash (glossy spot), zoom (blurred).
