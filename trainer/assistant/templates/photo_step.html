Guide: what to do, and why?
{% block guide %} {% if step_id == '5_tray' %}

What for? The dice tray is rebuilt in Blender: diameter and wall height determine the shape, the photos show colour and material of the wall. So the virtual tray looks like yours, and the dice lean against the wall in the same way.

  1. Enter the sizes below: diameter of the felt area inside, wall height from the felt to the top edge.
  2. Photo “from above”: whole tray, phone level and straight above it.
  3. Photo “from the side”: phone at tray height or slightly above, so that height and surface of the wall (glossy/matt, plastic/wood) are clearly visible. The felt need not be visible. The photo is for reference; more important is the wall height - measure it with a ruler and enter it below (it sets the height of the wall in Blender).

Pay attention to: no dice in the tray, even daylight, no flash, no zoom. Leave some margin around the tray.

Typical mistakes: at an angle from above instead of straight (then the tray is oval), flash makes glossy spots.

{% elif step_id == '6_felt' %}

What for? The close-up is laid onto the tray in Blender as a texture, so the virtual felt has the same colour and structure.

  1. Hold the phone about 20-30 cm above the felt, straight from above, only felt in the picture (no wall, no dice).
  2. Even daylight, no shadows of hand or phone, no flash.
  3. 1 photo is enough, up to 5 are possible (e.g. in different light).

Typical mistakes: own shadow in the picture, flash spot in the middle, tray wall in the picture.

{% elif step_id == '7_surroundings' %}

What for? Behind and next to the tray the camera sees your table and room while playing. These photos go into Blender as the background behind the virtual tray. So the detector learns that table, hands, cups etc. are no dice.

  1. 10-20 photos of where you play: table top, table edge, room in the background, from different directions.
  2. Everyday things are welcome: glasses, phone, paper, hands, a bit of mess too.
  3. Different light is good (daylight, lamp in the evening).

Pay attention to: no dice and no dice tray in these photos (it would confuse the training). Photos of people only if they agree.
