Firmware for the camera board

The firmware in {{ REPO|path('firmware') }} turns an ESP32-S3 camera board into a Wi-Fi camera: live view and single images from 640×480 up to 1600×1200, with auto or fixed exposure. The trainer fetches its images directly from the board. Once models are copied into firmware/models, the same firmware recognises the dice.

1. Build and flash

You need ESP-IDF (version 6.1 or newer), e.g. through the ESP-IDF extension of VS Code. In VS Code: open the folder firmware → in the status bar choose target esp32s3 and the serial port of the board → flame icon (build + flash + monitor).

Or in the ESP-IDF terminal:

cd "{{ REPO|path('firmware') }}"
idf.py set-target esp32s3
idf.py build flash monitor
  • The first build downloads the camera component and takes a few minutes.
  • set-target is only needed the first time.
  • If several serial ports exist, add -p <port> (e.g. -p COMx on Windows or -p /dev/ttyACM0 on Linux).
  • Quit the monitor: Ctrl+]

2. Set up the Wi-Fi (once)

  1. Without saved Wi-Fi the board opens its own hotspot Pokerdice (password pokerdice, see firmware/main/wifi_config.h). The LED is blue.
  2. Connect your phone or laptop to Pokerdice. Most phones open the page by themselves, otherwise open http://192.168.4.1.
  3. Tab Wi-Fi → choose your home Wi-Fi, enter the password → save. The board restarts.
  4. The LED blinks yellow while connecting and turns green when connected. The page is then at http://pokerdice.local (or at the IP shown in the serial log or your router).
  5. Enter the address in Step 0 · Setup → “Test camera board”.

The Wi-Fi credentials are stored only on the board (NVS), never in the code. Only 2.4 GHz networks work. If the home Wi-Fi is not reachable for 30 s, the board opens its hotspot as well (LED blue, blinking green) and keeps trying. To forget the home Wi-Fi, hold the BOOT button for 5 s (LED blinks white).

If green and red look swapped on your board, set LED_ORDER_RGB in firmware/main/boards/esp32s3_cam.h to the other value.

4. Directly in the browser

At http://pokerdice.local/ (in the hotspot http://192.168.4.1) the board has its own page:

  • Play - the poker hand and the five symbols, the live image with boxes (orange = “?”) and Save snapshot (camera image, detection as JSON and the image with the boxes).
  • History, Monitoring (time per step, memory, self-test of the models), Settings (threshold, stability, LED).
  • Camera - live view, single images, camera settings (JPEG quality, flip, brightness …). Live view also at http://pokerdice.local:81/stream.

Without models in firmware/models the board is a camera only (the page shows “No models”). While the trainer (or the Camera tab) uses the camera, the detection waits and continues 5 s later on its own.

CallMeaning
/api/infofirmware, sensor, resolutions, Wi-Fi, models (classes, self-test)
/api/statedetection: status, dice, hand, history, times
/api/framenewest camera image of the detection; header X-Detection = boxes and symbols of this image
/api/capture?res=1024x768single image: 640x480, 800x600, 1024x768, 1280x1024 or 1600x1200
/api/capture?exposure=-1auto exposure with correction −2 (darker) … +2 (brighter)
/api/capture?aec=300&gain=0fixed exposure time and gain 0…30 - the camera no longer adjusts

Without a value the last setting stays, also for the live view. The answer headers X-Exposure and X-Resolution tell what the sensor really used. Only one viewer can watch the live view at a time.

Common problems

What happensSolution
LED blinks redCamera not found. Look for “camera” in the monitor. Often: ribbon cable not fully inserted.
Image upside downOn the board's page switch “Flip vertically” / “Mirror” and save.
pokerdice.local not foundSome networks/systems do not resolve .local names. Use the IP address instead.
Page shows “No models”Choose the project on the page “Training & device” (models into firmware/models), then build and flash again.
Self-test “MISMATCH”The board computes the test image of the conversion slightly differently (often only rounding by 1 - the serial log shows the values). If the dice are still recognized, it is harmless; otherwise convert, build and flash again.
Live view in the trainer blackClose the other page with the live view (only one viewer) and reload.
