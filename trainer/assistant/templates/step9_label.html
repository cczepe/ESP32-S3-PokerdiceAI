Guide: what to do, and why?

What for? Each capture gets a box and the symbol per die. Only then can confusions be found later (e.g. “queen→jack 7×”), and errors that cancel each other out (K→A and A→K) become visible.

  1. Let “🤖 AI suggestions” be created for the next captures. This works in batches, Claude pre-determines the symbols.
  2. “Start labelling”: look at the capture, check each die, correct if needed, then Confirm (Enter).
  3. Target: {{ target_min }}-{{ target_max }} confirmed captures. You can continue later at any time.
    10 are enough for a first impression; for reliable numbers (e.g. to tell 95 % from 99 %) rather 100.

Rules for labelling:

  • Symbol = what lies on top.
  • “?” = the die is tilted, or the top symbol really cannot be determined.
  • Spot is no die or several dice → choose “no die” and draw the dice one by one.
  • Half hidden dice at the image border: draw them if the symbol can be determined, otherwise “?”.
