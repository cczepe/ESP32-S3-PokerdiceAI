Guide: what to do, and why?

What for? These captures are the real test images. Only they show how good the recognition really is. Synthetic images alone deceive (a model can reach 99 % on synthetic test images and still show weaknesses on real camera images). They are also used to check and fine-tune the models.

  1. Throw, leave the dice, hold the camera board as when playing.
  2. Capture (button or space bar). Without AI, this is quick.
  3. Throw again … Target: {{ target_min }}-{{ target_max }} captures. Several sessions are fine, the count continues.
    10 are enough for a first impression; for reliable numbers (e.g. to tell 95 % from 99 %) rather 100.
  4. Labelling follows in step 9.

Variety matters - please mix deliberately:

  • close and further away, steeper and flatter{% if cs and cs.elevation_deg %} (your camera setup: {{ cs.elevation_deg.min }}°-{{ cs.elevation_deg.max }}°{% if cs.distance_cm %}, {{ cs.distance_cm.min }}-{{ cs.distance_cm.max }} cm{% endif %}){% endif %}
  • different light: daylight, lamp, weaker in the evening
  • dice that touch each other or lie at the wall
  • now and then a die tilted (leaning against the wall) - that later becomes “?”
  • all symbols often enough, not only “nice” throws

Please with the camera board, not with the phone: different sharpness, colours, noise and a different lens.
