Guide: what to do, and why?

What for? A 3D die in real size is built in Blender. So that it looks just as large and just as “round” on the renders as yours, we need three values.

  1. Edge length: measure from face to face with a caliper (most accurate) or a ruler, best at 2-3 places, average. Poker dice are usually 15-19 mm.
  2. Corner radius: how much edges and corners are rounded, as a radius in mm. An estimate is enough: sharp edges ≈ 0.5 mm, clearly round ≈ 1.5-2.5 mm, very round ≈ 3 mm. More exact: lay a ruler flat on a face and look against the light - the rounding starts where light comes through; or radius = (edge length − width of the flat face) / 2.
  3. Body color: colour of the die body (without symbols), e.g. ivory, white, cream.

Typical mistakes: measuring over the rounded edges (too small) or at an angle (too large).
