What for? Poker dice are not standardised. Which symbol lies next to which and how it is rotated differs between manufacturers. The 3D die in Blender must match yours exactly. Otherwise the model sees neighbour combinations in training that never occur in reality, and learns wrong hints from the side faces.

  1. 4 corner photos with the phone: at an angle onto a corner, so that 3 faces are equally well visible.
    • Photo 1 and 2: two opposite corners of the top face (e.g. front-right and back-left).
    • Turn the die over. Photo 3 and 4: again two opposite corners.
    So each face is seen at least once with its neighbours.
  2. “🤖 Suggest layout” fills in the die net from the photos.
  3. Correct it in the net: choose a symbol, rotate with ↺ ↻. Turn the 3D preview on the right with the corner buttons to the same corner as a photo and compare: same symbols, same rotation?
  4. Cross-check directly on the real die: enter “what lies opposite?” for each symbol.
  5. When everything matches: “Confirm layout”.

The die net: the die unfolded, seen from outside. Opposite lie top-bottom, front-back, left-right.

Rotation: a rotation is recorded for pips (9, 10) and the ace too - compare closely with the corner photos (e.g. in which direction the rows of pips run).

Typical mistakes: only corners of one side photographed (the bottom is then missing), corner not centred (one face almost invisible).
